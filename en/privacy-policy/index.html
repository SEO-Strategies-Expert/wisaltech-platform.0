WORKING DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION

1. Who We Are

Wisal Tech is a virtual digital marketing, creative and technology company serving clients in Qatar and selected regional markets. References to “Wisal Tech”, “we”, “us” or “our” in this policy refer to the entity operating the website at wisaltech.agency.

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through the website, enquiry forms, email, WhatsApp and other communication channels linked from the website. Separate agreements may apply to client projects, employees, suppliers or third-party platforms.

3. Information We May Collect

We may collect your name, company, job title, email address, phone number, project details, service interests, budget range, messages and any files or information you choose to provide. We may also collect limited technical information such as device type, browser, approximate location, pages viewed, referral source and interaction events through approved analytics tools.

4. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, understand project needs, prepare proposals, deliver services, manage client communication, improve the website, measure marketing performance, protect our systems, meet legal obligations and maintain appropriate business records.

5. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required, we rely on your consent, steps requested before entering an agreement, performance of an agreement, compliance with legal obligations or other lawful grounds available under applicable law. You may withdraw consent for optional communications by contacting us, without affecting processing already completed lawfully.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential technologies required for operation and, where enabled, analytics or marketing technologies that help us understand usage and campaign performance. Non-essential technologies should be activated only through an appropriate consent mechanism where required.

7. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with authorized team members, professional advisers, hosting providers, communication services, analytics providers, project tools and other processors required to operate the website or deliver the requested service. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

8. International Processing

Because Wisal Tech operates virtually and may use service providers in different countries, information may be processed outside Qatar. Where this occurs, we will seek appropriate contractual, technical and organizational safeguards as required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including enquiry follow-up, service delivery, record keeping, dispute management and legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by the type of information and relationship.

10. Information Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through general enquiry channels.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent or information about how your personal data is handled. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

12. Third-Party Links

The website may link to third-party websites, social networks, messaging services or tools. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Wisal Tech is not responsible for content or processing controlled by those third parties.

13. Children

The website and services are intended for business users and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the general website.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in services, technology or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

15. Contact Us

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to hello@wisaltech.com or through the contact details published on the website. Please include enough information for us to understand and respond to the request.
