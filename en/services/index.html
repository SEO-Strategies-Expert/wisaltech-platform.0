COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we start with one service?

Yes. Many engagements begin with one defined need, such as a company profile, website, SEO audit or campaign. We can connect additional services only when they support the same objective.

How do you define the project scope?

We begin with the business goal, current situation, required deliverables, review points and timeline. The agreed proposal then becomes the reference for delivery.

Do you work with clients outside Qatar?

Yes. Wisal Tech operates through a virtual delivery model and has experience supporting projects across several regional markets.
